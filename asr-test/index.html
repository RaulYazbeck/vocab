How this works

  1. Tap Start once. The mic then stays on for the whole session.
  2. You see an English word. Say the German word, with its article ("der Tisch"), exactly as you would in the app.
  3. It detects the end of your answer and moves on by itself. Think, hesitate, say "ähm" — behave normally. If you don't know it, tap Skip. Don't retry words to make them pass.
  4. After 50 words: Export the .json and send it to Claude. That file is what matters.
